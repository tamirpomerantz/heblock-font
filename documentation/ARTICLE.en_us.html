Heblock is a geometric sans serif for Latin and Hebrew. The Latin is based on DM Sans by Colophon Foundry. Tamir Pomerantz designed the Hebrew, adjusted selected Latin letters, and produced the family.

The family has static weights from Thin (100) to Black (900). Hebrew includes niqqud (vowel points and dagesh) with OpenType mark attachment. It is intended for UI and text use in mixed Hebrew and Latin settings.

To contribute, see github.com/tamirpomerantz/heblock.
